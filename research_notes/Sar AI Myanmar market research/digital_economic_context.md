# Myanmar digital and economic conditions for a paid Android health app (Sar AI), as of Nov 2026

Research date: 1 Nov 2026. Each figure carries the date of the underlying data. "OLD" marks data from before 2024. Where a source's page date and article date may differ (common with DVB / Irrawaddy mirrors), that is flagged.

## 1. Devices, smartphone penetration, Android vs iOS, subscriptions and internet users

### Takeaway
About 72.5% of Myanmar's 54.9M people (39.8M) used the internet in Oct 2025, and there were 62.5M cellular connections (114% of population), nearly all 3G/4G-capable. Android accounts for about 80-84% of mobile web traffic (mid-2026), so an Android-first launch covers the large majority of users. iOS (about 16-20%) is a bigger minority than the "Android-only market" stereotype suggests. TikTok, not Facebook, now has the widest ad reach.

### Cited Findings
- Population 54.9M (Oct 2025); 33.0% urban / 67.0% rural; median age 30.1 — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Internet users 39.8M in Oct 2025 (72.5% penetration), +1.9M (+4.9%) year on year; 15.1M (27.5%) offline — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Cellular mobile connections (GSMA Intelligence) 62.5M at end-2025 = 114% of population, down 1.3M (-2.0%) year on year; 96.8% of connections are "broadband" (3G/4G/5G) — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- DataReportal warns that connections exceed population because of multi-SIM use, that broadband capability does not equal internet use, and that year-on-year changes should not be calculated against earlier reports because of method revisions — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Earlier figure for comparison: 24.11M internet users at start of 2024 (different method, not comparable) — [DataReportal Digital 2024: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-myanmar?rq=myanmar)
- Social media user identities: 21.0M (38.2% of population) in Oct 2025, +3.0M (+16.5%) year on year; 61.2% male / 38.8% female (18+) — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Ad reach, Oct 2025 / late 2025: TikTok 21.0M adults (53.6% of adults); Facebook 13.7M (24.9% of population), +7.9% year on year; Messenger 8.2M; Instagram 929K (1.7%); LinkedIn 1.1M — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Facebook ad reach is roughly 50/50 by gender, while TikTok's is about 61% male — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Median fixed download speed 26.90 Mbps (Ookla, to Aug 2025), +24.8% in 12 months. The page does not give a median mobile download speed — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)
- Mobile OS share (StatCounter, web traffic): Android 84.0% / iOS 15.99% (Jul 2026); Android 80.51% / iOS 19.45% (Jun 2026). Mobile+tablet: Android 83.44% / iOS 16.55% (Jul 2026) — [StatCounter Myanmar mobile OS](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile/myanmar); [StatCounter mobile+tablet](https://gs.statcounter.com/os-market-share/mobile-tablet/myanmar)
- Android version distribution is available at [StatCounter Android version, Myanmar](https://gs.statcounter.com/android-version-market-share/mobile-os/myanmar) (not extracted in this session)

### Inferences
- A 2.0% drop in mobile connections alongside rising internet users suggests SIM consolidation (likely tied to SIM registration enforcement and consumers dropping secondary SIMs), not falling usage. This is not confirmed by the source.
- StatCounter measures web page views, which over-represent urban and wealthier (iOS-heavier) users. The real share of Android handsets is probably above 84%.
- With 61% male skew on TikTok and about 50/50 on Facebook, Facebook remains the better channel for reaching women. Women are a likely core audience for a calorie tracker, though this is an inference.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 source found for smartphone ownership rate (share of people or connections on smartphones vs feature phones), typical brands (e.g. Xiaomi/Redmi, Samsung, Oppo, Vivo, Realme, Tecno), typical RAM, or handset price bands in Myanmar. GSMA Mobile Economy Asia Pacific and ITU country figures were not retrieved.
- No median mobile speed figure for Myanmar (Ookla) was found in the DataReportal 2026 page.
- Unique mobile subscribers (vs connections) from GSMA was not retrieved.

## 2. Mobile data cost, operators, network quality, SIM registration and tax

### Takeaway
Since Sept 2025 four operators have run the market: MPT, ATOM (ex-Telenor), U9 (ex-Ooredoo) and Mytel. In late 2025 they competed on SIM prices and bundled data, but no reliable 2025-2026 per-GB price list was found. Indicative traveller-guide prices are about 1,500-3,900 MMK per GB (roughly US$0.35-1.85 at official rates, much less in dollar terms at market rates).

### Cited Findings
- Since Sept 2025 the mobile market has four operators: MPT, U9 (formerly Ooredoo), Mytel and ATOM — [Japan MIC (soumu) Myanmar ICT profile](https://www.soumu.go.jp/g-ict/country/myanmar/detail.html)
- From Q3 2025 operators pushed SIM sales, cut SIM prices and gave away large data allowances to compete. Before that shift, Mytel had about 50-60% of SIM sales, ATOM 20-30%, and U9 and MPT about 10% each (industry representative quoted in Vietnamese press; this is a sales share, not a subscriber share) — [VnEconomy](https://vneconomy.vn/viettel-va-thi-truong-myanmar-bay-gio-canh-tranh-moi-khoc-liet.htm)
- Indicative prices (undated eSIM/traveller guide, low reliability): MPT 1 GB for 3,889 Ks (about US$1.85); ATOM 5 GB for 7,500 Ks (about US$3.60), 30 days — [Roami Myanmar eSIM carrier guide](https://www.roamiapp.com/ja/carriers/myanmar-esim-carrier-guide/)
- MPT publishes its base tariff at [MPT Base Tariff](https://mpt.com.mm/en/base-tariff/) (not extracted)
- OLD (2021): data prices rose by about half after the coup, as the value of data packs shrank — [RFA Burmese, Dec 2021](https://www.rfa.org/burmese/news/phone-internet-data-price-rise-12082021032912.html)
- OLD (2018): price war drove data to about 1 kyat per MB — [Internet in Myanmar](https://www.internetinmyanmar.com/articles/mobile-operator-myanmar-drop-data-price/); [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/telenor/myanmars-newcomers-kick-data-price-war/)

### Inferences
- In late 2025 the trend shifted to operators competing on volume, so data is probably not the binding cost for a small app (Sar AI's APK plus occasional sync and photo uploads). The bigger risk is coverage loss in conflict areas (see section 3).
- At about 2,000-4,000 MMK per GB, a 30-50 MB app download costs well under 200 MMK. Photo-scan uploads should still be compressed for users on small daily packs.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 source on the post-coup SIM card and internet taxes (the 2021-2022 increases in SIM card tax and data tax) or current enforcement of SIM registration and SIM deactivation.
- No Cable.co.uk / ITU affordability figure (cost of 1 GB as % of GNI per capita) retrieved for 2025.
- No independent network-quality comparison (Opensignal/Ookla) by operator for 2025-2026.
- No reliability data on application-to-person (A2P) SMS / OTP delivery to +95 numbers. An SMS pricing page exists ([sent.dm Myanmar SMS pricing](https://www.sent.dm/en/resources/sms-pricing/myanmar-sms-pricing)) but was not reviewed. Shutdown areas (section 3) also cut voice/SMS, so OTP-only login will fail for users in those townships.

## 3. Internet shutdowns, social media blocks, VPNs, Google services

### Takeaway
Myanmar is one of the most restricted internet environments in the world: Freedom House scored it 9/100 in 2025. There were 105 shutdown incidents across 73 townships in 2025, mostly in conflict regions (Sagaing, Magway, Tanintharyi, Ayeyarwady). The Cybersecurity Law (in force 30 Jul 2025) criminalises unregistered VPN services, phone checks for VPNs followed, and a broad VPN block was reported in Nov 2025. No source reports that Google Play itself is blocked.

### Cited Findings
- Freedom on the Net 2025: Myanmar scores 9/100, among the world's worst alongside China. The 2025 report was abridged because of budget constraints — [Progressive Voice summary of FOTN 2025](https://progressivevoicemyanmar.org/2025/11/13/myanmar-freedom-on-the-net-2025); [Freedom House Myanmar FOTN 2025](https://freedomhouse.org/index%2Ephp/country/myanmar/freedom-net/2025)
- 2025: 105 shutdown instances in 73 townships across 14 states and regions. 47.6% were further mobile-service cuts in areas already shut down, 15.2% internet-only shutdowns, and 39.1% total internet plus mobile termination (the shares sum to 101.9% in the source). Magway, Sagaing and Tanintharyi each had more than 10 instances, and Ayeyarwady had the most townships (9) — [Myanmar Internet Project, 2025 yearly report](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)
- Armed groups also restricted connectivity 13 times in 2025, citing airstrike risk in over 40% of cases — [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)
- 2024: Access Now counted Myanmar as the global leader with 85 deliberate disruptions — cited in [Progressive Voice / FOTN 2025 summary](https://progressivevoicemyanmar.org/2025/11/13/myanmar-freedom-on-the-net-2025)
- May 2024: a VPN ban left many providers unable to connect and blocked access to social apps and websites — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/vpns/vpns-are-under-attack-in-myanmar-as-new-security-law-is-introduced)
- Cybersecurity Law No. 1/2025 was enacted 1 Jan 2025 and brought into force 30 Jul 2025 (Notification 113/2025) — [RFA](https://rfa.org/english/myanmar/2025/01/02/cybersecurity-law-vpn/); [Rahmat Lim & Partners](https://rahmatlim.com/publication/articles/31053/cybersecurity-law-in-force-30-july-2025)
- VPN penalty: 1-6 months' prison and/or a 1-10M MMK fine for unregistered VPN services (Clause 70). Mobile phone checks for VPN use followed in several areas after the law took effect — [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1). Sources conflict on fine size (another cites up to 2M MMK) and on whether users or only providers are liable: the law firm reads it as regulating providers, while VOA/AP describe user liability — [RFA](https://rfa.org/english/myanmar/2025/01/02/cybersecurity-law-vpn/); [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/myanmar-issues-cybersecurity-law/6/)
- The law requires service providers to retain user data for 3 years — [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)
- The law reaches Myanmar citizens abroad — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/myanmar-issues-cybersecurity-law/6/)
- Nov 2025: the junta intensified the crackdown with a VPN block. Human Rights Myanmar describes the VPN block combined with the Facebook ban as possibly "the largest act of censorship" in recent Myanmar history — [Internet in Myanmar digest headline, Nov 2025](https://www.internetinmyanmar.com/digest/2025-11-14-myanmars-junta-intensifies-internet-crackdown-with-vpn-block/); [Human Rights Myanmar reports](https://humanrightsmyanmar.org/category/reports/)
- Arrests: 197 people arrested for social media activity in 2025 (77.7% for Facebook, 21.8% for TikTok), and 1,993 jailed for social media activity from Feb 2022 to Oct 2025 (Data for Myanmar). Reports name a "Tiangou Secure Gateway" surveillance system — [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)
- Estimated economic loss from shutdowns since the 2021 coup: nearly US$500M (ISOC NetLoss) — [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)
- Facebook ad reach still grew 7.9% year on year to 13.7M (Oct 2025) despite the block, which implies continued access via VPNs — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Myanmar](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-myanmar)

### Inferences
- Facebook, Instagram and X have been officially blocked since Feb 2021 (widely reported; not re-verified with a primary source this session). Facebook's 13.7M ad reach shows mass VPN use. The Nov 2025 VPN block and phone checks make Facebook marketing riskier for users and less reliable, while TikTok (not blocked, per the arrest data showing open TikTok use) gains as a channel.
- Shutdowns are concentrated in conflict townships in Sagaing, Magway, Tanintharyi, Rakhine and similar areas, not in Yangon, Mandalay or Naypyitaw. Urban target users usually keep connectivity, but Sar AI's offline-first design is necessary rather than optional.
- Because shutdowns often cut mobile voice/SMS too, phone-OTP sign-in and server-granted passes need an offline grace period.
- Health and diet data on a foreign server is a sensitive category under a law requiring 3-year data retention and allowing state access. Minimal data collection and on-device storage reduce exposure.

### Gaps
- No direct source confirming the status of Google Play Store, Google Play Services, Firebase/FCM push, Google Sign-In or YouTube in Myanmar in 2025-2026. No report of them being blocked was found, but absence of evidence is not confirmation.
- No quantitative VPN usage rate (e.g. % of internet users) for 2025-2026.
- Access Now's 2025 #KeepItOn count for Myanmar was not retrieved.
- Status of X/Instagram/WhatsApp/Signal individually in 2026 is not confirmed.

## 4. Mobile money, cards and Google Play Billing in Myanmar

### Takeaway
KBZPay is the dominant wallet (about 22M users, or about 19M active at end-2024) and transaction volume nearly doubled in 2025, so a KBZPay/WavePay prepaid pass flow is aligned with how people actually pay. Wave Money offers an online merchant gateway ("Pay with Wave"). Google Play lists Myanmar as a paid-app country with MMK pricing, but its payment-methods page offers only international cards for unlisted countries like Myanmar, so Play Billing is a weak fallback for most users.

### Cited Findings
- KBZPay: about 22M total users, close to 60% of adults. Annual transactions rose from 2.1B (2024) to more than 3.8B (2025) — [Huawei case study: KBZPay](https://e.huawei.com/my/case-studies/industries/finance/kbzpay)
- KBZPay: 19M active users = 43% of adult population, end-2024 — [FinanceAsia, KBZ Bank 2025 award spotlight](https://www.financeasia.com/article/winner-spotlight-2025-kbz-bank/503374). The two figures are probably total vs active users, but neither source states that.
- Wave Money's "Pay with Wave" online merchant gateway (Digital Money Myanmar Ltd) supports a merchant ID plus secret key, HTTPS API, merchant portal, settlement, transaction history and real-time payment notifications. Onboarding with Wave Money is required — [Wave Money: Pay with Wave](https://wavemoney.com.mm/partner/pay-with-wave); [Wave partner docs](https://partners.wavemoney.com.mm:443/documentation)
- Google Play developer country table: Myanmar supports paid apps, in-app purchases and subscriptions, with buyer prices from MMK 100 to MMK 719,000 — [Google Play Console Help: supported locations](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/138294)
- Google Play "Accepted payment methods" page: Myanmar is **not** in the country list, so the "other countries" fallback applies: credit/debit cards only (Amex, Discover, Mastercard, Visa). No carrier billing, gift cards, e-wallets or Play balance are listed for Myanmar — [Google Play Help: accepted payment methods](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2651410?hl=en)
- OLD / undated: Ooredoo Myanmar announced it was the first Myanmar operator to offer direct carrier billing for Google Play. Ooredoo has since become U9, and current availability is unconfirmed — [Mizzima](https://mizzima.com/node/9658)

### Inferences
- Card penetration is low and cross-border card payments are constrained by FX controls, so Google Play Billing will reach mainly a small, card-holding (often diaspora or urban-elite) segment. KBZPay/WavePay should be the primary rail, consistent with the product plan.
- Google Play policy normally requires Play Billing for digital goods consumed in-app. Selling passes via KBZPay/WavePay inside the Android app may conflict with Play's payments policy unless a local exception or external-offer programme applies to Myanmar. This needs checking against current Play policy, and was not verified here.

### Gaps
- No WavePay user numbers (2024-2026) found.
- No published merchant/MDR fees for KBZPay or WavePay online merchant payments found.
- No 2025-2026 data on debit/credit card penetration in Myanmar (World Bank Findex / CBM).
- Whether Google Play gift cards are sold locally, or U9/MPT/ATOM carrier billing works in 2026, is unconfirmed.
- Whether Google pays out to developers in Myanmar, or whether a Myanmar merchant account is needed, was not researched.

## 5. Economy since the 2021 coup: growth, inflation, kyat, poverty, electricity

### Takeaway
The economy is "stabilising at low levels". GDP shrank about 2% in FY2025/26 and is forecast to grow 2% in FY2026/27. Inflation spiked back to about 25% in April 2026 on a Hormuz-related fuel shock. Poverty was 29.9% in 2025. The kyat trades at about 2,100/USD officially but about 3,700-4,600/USD in market channels (mid-2026). Yangon households report only 2-10 hours of grid power a day. Discretionary spending power is weak, so pass prices must be small and in kyat.

### Cited Findings
- World Bank Myanmar Economic Monitor (June 2026): inflation peaked at 24.6% y/y in April 2026, and was 21.1% for the 12 months to end-March 2026. The FY2026/27 growth forecast was cut to 2% (from 3%). Poverty was 29.9% in 2025, "still far above pre-2021 trends". About 90% of fuel oil is imported. The shock followed the US-Iran war from 28 Feb 2026 and the Strait of Hormuz closure — [Myanmar Now / AFP, 16 Jun 2026](https://myanmar-now.org/en/news/myanmar-inflation-hits-25-on-us-iran-fuel-shock-world-bank/)
- Real GDP estimated to have contracted by 2% in FY2025/26, with the economy "stabilising at low levels" (World Bank, June 2026) — [fundsforNGOs summary of WB release, 17 Jun 2026](https://news.fundsforngos.org/2026/06/17/myanmar-economy-shows-signs-of-stabilisation-but-faces-growing-fuel-pressures/); [World Bank Myanmar](https://www.worldbank.org/en/where-we-work/myanmar)
- Conflicting poverty figure: one outlet reports poverty exceeded 32% "early this year" (a 2015-level), but the edition and date are unclear — [Irrawaddy](https://www.irrawaddy.com/news/burma/world-banks-latest-report-myanmar-5-takeaways-growth-risks.html)
- June 2025 World Bank: the March 2025 earthquake compounded Myanmar's economic challenges — [World Bank press release, 12 Jun 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/12/earthquake-compounds-myanmar-s-economic-challenges)
- Kyat, official vs market (all 2026):
  - CBM reference rate 2,100 MMK/USD, against a CBM-reported weighted average trading rate of about 3,700-3,777 (June 2026, via aggregator) — [regalert.today CBM bulletin](https://regalert.today/document/cbm-reference-rate-and-trading-turnover-data-d3d8d231-7b48-4487-9455-1e3245154a70/text)
  - Official USD/MMK 2,093.70 (10 Apr 2026) — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/myanmar/currency/forecast); 2,099.7 (31 Jul 2026) — [ValutaFX](https://www.valutafx.com/history/usd-mmk-2026-07-31)
  - Black market about 4,460-4,610 MMK/USD (3 Aug 2026, low-reliability aggregator) — [egcurrency](https://egcurrency.com/en/currency/USD-to-MMK/blackMarket)
  - Conflicting lower readings: black market 2,900-3,000 per USD "as of August 1" (DVB, article year uncertain) — [DVB](https://english.dvb.no/black-market-dollar-price-rises-to-3000-kyat/); about 2,500 open market vs about 1,860 official in Rakhine (June 2026, regional) — [DMG weekly highlights](https://mail.dmediag.com/news/mm-thai-mon.html)
  - From 1 Jan 2025, customs duties are assessed at the weekly market trading rate rather than the CBM reference rate — [Rahmat Lim & Partners](https://www.rahmatlim.com/publication/articles/29839/payment-of-taxes-on-imports-and-exports-in-kyat-at-market-trading-rate-from-1-january-2025)
  - The kyat has lost about three-quarters of its value under junta rule (headline) — [bne IntelliNews](https://www.intellinews.com/myanmar-s-banks-buckle-under-junta-rule-as-the-kyat-loses-three-quarters-of-its-value-461180/)
- Electricity: national generation meets about 35% of demand, with prolonged outages in Yangon and Mandalay (Mar 2026). The ministry promised 14 hours/day from 22 Apr 2026, but some regions get only 8-10 hours (Aug 2026). In Oct 2026 Yangon residents report about 2 hours/day in some townships. Sources are exile/opposition media relying on resident accounts — [BNI](https://www.bnionline.net/en/node/107906); [BNI](https://www.bnionline.net/en/node/108001); [Irrawaddy](https://www.irrawaddy.com/news/burma/life-without-power-spells-daily-misery-for-yangons-residents.html); [Myanmar Now (likely Jan 2025)](https://myanmar-now.org/en/news/myanmar-suffers-worsening-power-cuts-as-junta-prioritizes-military-zones/)

### Inferences
- Pricing should be set in kyat and reviewed often. A 3,000-5,000 MMK monthly pass is about US$1.40-2.40 at the official rate but only about US$0.65-1.10 at the market rate, so Google Play's MMK price tiers (pegged to Google's own FX) may look expensive or cheap relative to local wallet prices.
- Power cuts cause phone-charging and cell-tower outages (towers run on diesel, which became more expensive in the 2026 fuel shock). Low battery use and offline logging matter.
- With about 30% poverty and about 25% inflation, the paying segment is mainly urban, salaried or remittance-receiving households, a minority of the 39.8M internet users.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 figure for household income, wages, urban middle-class size, or consumer spending on digital services/apps.
- IMF and ADB 2026 forecasts not retrieved. The ADB Asian Development Outlook figures for Myanmar would add a second view.
- Latest (post-June 2026) inflation reading not found.

## 6. Political situation, sanctions and data-law compliance

### Takeaway
After phased junta-run elections (Dec 2025-Jan 2026), coup leader Min Aung Hlaing was elected president by a USDP-dominated parliament on 3 Apr 2026, so the military still effectively governs. US sanctions target the Ministry of Defense and the state banks MFTB and MICB (since June 2023). Consumer app sales paid via private wallets (KBZ, Wave) are not themselves sanctioned, but the counterparties need screening. The Cybersecurity Law imposes data retention and VPN rules. No standalone personal or health data protection law was found.

### Cited Findings
- Junta election held in phases about Dec 2025-Jan 2026. Min Aung Hlaing gave up military command (General Ye Win Oo appointed), was elected president by parliament on 3 Apr 2026 with 429 of 584 votes, and was sworn in about 10 Apr 2026. Vice-presidents are Nyo Saw and Nan Ni Ni Aye — [NST, Apr 2026](https://www.nst.com.my/amp/world/region/2026/04/1410016/myanmar-junta-chief-elected-president-pro-military-mps); [Cambodianess](https://cambodianess.com/article/myanmar-parliament-to-elect-new-president-friday-speaker); [Cape Argus, 2 Apr 2026](https://capeargus.co.za/news/world/2026-04-02-myanmar-parliament-to-elect-new-president-friday-speaker)
- Electoral Law (29 Jul 2025) clauses carry 3-7 years' prison, and 34 people were charged under it in 2025 — [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)
- US sanctions: on 21 Jun 2023 OFAC designated Burma's Ministry of Defense, Myanma Foreign Trade Bank (MFTB) and Myanma Investment and Commercial Bank (MICB) under E.O. 14014, with General License 5 for wind-down — [US Treasury press release jy1555](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/jy1555); [Willkie compliance note](https://complianceconcourse.willkie.com/articles/us-designates-burmas-ministry-of-defense-and-two-burmese-financial-institutions/)
- Myanma Economic Bank (MEB) is not among the 2023 designations. OLD (2016): MEB, MFTB and MICB were all delisted in May 2016 before the 2023 redesignation of two of them — [US-ASEAN Business Council](https://www.usasean.org/article/us-treasury-announces-sanctions-against-two-myanmar-state-owned-banks); [Norton Rose Fulbright](https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en-us/knowledge/publications/d37e9f70/ofac-amends-burma-myanmar-sanctions)
- Cybersecurity Law 2025: covers digital platform service providers, criminalises unregistered VPN services, requires 3-year user-data retention, and has extraterritorial reach to citizens — [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/myanmar-issues-cybersecurity-law/6/); [Rahmat Lim](https://rahmatlim.com/publication/articles/31053/cybersecurity-law-in-force-30-july-2025); [Myanmar Internet Project](https://www.myanmarinternet.info/post/yearly_report_2025-1)

### Inferences
- For a foreign developer, the main sanctions exposure is the payment chain: avoid settlement through MFTB/MICB or military-linked entities, and screen KBZ Bank, Wave Money and their owners against OFAC/EU/UK lists before contracting. KBZ and Wave are private and were not found on sanctions lists in this research, but that was not verified against the SDN list.
- If Sar AI's Cybersecurity Law obligations apply (e.g. as a "digital platform service" with a large Myanmar user base), registration and data-retention duties could arise. Keeping health data on-device and minimal on the server reduces risk. A local lawyer should confirm.

### Gaps
- Whether the US, EU or UK changed Myanmar sanctions after the 2026 "civilian" transition was not confirmed. A Steptoe note titled "Limited US sanctions relief after historic Myanmar elections" exists but could not be fetched (403) and may concern 2015-2016 — [Steptoe](https://www.steptoe.com/en/news-publications/limited-us-sanctions-relief-after-historic-myanmar-elections.html)
- EU restrictive measures (e.g. on Myanma Oil and Gas Enterprise (MOGE), the state oil and gas company) and any EU/UK listing of banks were not retrieved.
- Whether KBZ Bank or Wave Money (Yoma/Telenor/First Myanmar Investment) owners are on any sanctions list was not checked.
- No Myanmar personal data protection law or health-data law was identified. The Electronic Transactions Law amendments and the 2017 Law Protecting the Privacy and Security of Citizens (whose privacy sections were suspended post-coup) were not researched.

## 7. Thailand comparison (next market)

### Takeaway
Thailand is a much richer and more open market: 67.8M internet users (Oct 2025, about 94.7% penetration) and 96.6M mobile connections (135%). PromptPay (QR/bank transfer) and TrueMoney are the dominant local payment rails, and Codapay estimates US$1.1B in app revenue in 2025. Subscription habits specific to health apps were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Internet users 67.8M in Oct 2025, +2.4M (+3.7%) year on year — [DataReportal Digital 2026: Thailand](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-thailand)
- Internet penetration 94.7%. Cellular connections 96.6M at end-2025 = 135% of population (GSMA Intelligence via DataReportal, repeated by an agency) — [Elite Asia, Digital trends Thailand 2026](https://www.eliteasia.co/digital-and-social-media-trends-in-thailand-in-2026/)

### Inferences
- In Thailand Google Play Billing (cards, carrier billing, and likely TrueMoney/e-wallet top-up) becomes a viable primary rail, unlike Myanmar. There is also a large Myanmar migrant population in Thailand who could be a bridge audience for a Burmese-language app. That audience size was not researched here.

### Gaps
- Thailand Android vs iOS share, which Google Play payment methods are listed for Thailand, and health/fitness app subscription rates were not retrieved.
- Size of the Myanmar migrant worker population in Thailand not researched.
