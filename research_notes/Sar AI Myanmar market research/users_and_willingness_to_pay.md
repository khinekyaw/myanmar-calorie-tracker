# Sar AI: Likely Users in Myanmar, Their Motivations, and Willingness to Pay

Research date: 2026-11-01. Price under test: 4,900 MMK per month or 12,900 MMK per 3 months for a prepaid Premium pass (free tier: 3 AI photo scans a day, unlimited voice, text and search logging).

**Exchange rates used in these notes (all MMK per USD):**
- **Official/CBM reference rate:** 2,100 (CBM reference page, 13 Oct 2026). Aggregators show 2,093.7–2,099.8 through 2026.
- **CBM "market trading rate":** 3,660 (same CBM page, 13 Oct 2026).
- **Informal/street rate:** about 4,600–4,700 (MoeMaKa, 16 Oct 2026). These notes use a midpoint of 4,650.

Sources: [CBM reference rate](https://forex.cbm.gov.mm/index.php/fxrate); [MoeMaKa, Oct 2026](https://moemaka.net/eng/2026/10/myanmar-kyat-depreciates-amid-growing-instability-raising-concerns-over-rising-consumer-prices/); [ValutaFX 31 Jul 2026](https://www.valutafx.com/history/usd-mmk-2026-07-31); [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/myanmar/currency). The CBM figures came through a search summary of the CBM page, not a direct fetch, so check them on the live page.

## 1. Who in Myanmar cares about weight, diet and fitness?

### Takeaway
I found no market study that profiles Myanmar's diet or fitness consumers. The strongest evidence is health burden. About 1 in 10 adults has diabetes and about 1 in 5 has prediabetes, both more common in women. Hypertension and abdominal obesity are common in Yangon. The private wellness market (supplements, "beauty-from-within") is urban, centred on Yangon and Mandalay, led by middle-aged women, sold through Facebook and Telegram, and price-sensitive. The most defensible first segments are urban women aged about 25–55 who care about weight and appearance, and adults with diabetes or hypertension plus the family members who cook for them. Men's fitness has no data behind it.

### Cited Findings
- **Diabetes (2014 national STEPS survey):** prevalence was 10.5% overall, 9.1% in men and 11.8% in women — [PMC6765960 (Yangon trends paper citing STEPS 2014)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6765960/). A separate 2014 national analysis reported 10.8% overall (men 11.5%, women 9.2%), so the two papers disagree on which sex is higher — [Diabetes, Metabolic Syndrome and Obesity, 2019 (DOAJ)](https://doaj.org/article/3250112488fd49c8af25d1e1d00a9145).
- **Prediabetes (2014 national survey):** 19.7% overall, 16.5% in men and 23% in women — [DOAJ companion paper](https://doaj.org/article/3250112488fd49c8af25d1e1d00a9145).
- **Diabetes in Yangon Region (age-standardised):** 8.3% in 2004 and 10.2% in 2014. The rise was not statistically significant (p = 0.296) — [PMC6765960](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6765960/).
- **IDF Diabetes Atlas:** age-standardised prevalence of 7.6% for its latest year shown, based on STEPS 2014. It projects about 3.3 million adults with diabetes by 2050 — [IDF Diabetes Atlas, Myanmar](https://diabetesatlas.org/data-by-location/country/myanmar/).
- **Hypertension in Yangon Region (age-standardised):** rose from 26.7% in 2004 to 34.6% in 2014 — [BMC Public Health via duo.uio.no](https://www.duo.uio.no/handle/10852/59030).
- **Raised blood pressure, ages 40–64 (2014 STEPS subsample):** 41.9% — [IJCMPH secondary analysis](https://www.ijcmph.com/index.php/ijcmph/article/download/11032/6654/49716).
- **Metabolic syndrome:** one study put prevalence at 29.1%, with abdominal obesity the most common component — [MDPI IJERPH 16(18):3461](https://www.mdpi.com/1660-4601/16/18/3461).
- **Overweight and obesity:** STEPS 2014 measured adults aged 25–64 (sample of 8,757 for obesity) using WHO BMI cut-offs. I could not retrieve the prevalence figures themselves — [World Obesity data](https://data.worldobesity.org/country/Myanmar-148/country/myanmar-148/actions.pdf).
- **Supplements and wellness market (US Commerce Dept, page dated 17 Sep 2026):**
  - Urban consumers in Yangon and Mandalay focus on health maintenance and prevention.
  - "Beauty-from-within" products such as collagen remain particularly strong among middle-aged women.
  - Social commerce through Facebook, Telegram and local delivery is a major driver of sales.
  - Consumers are price-sensitive and prefer smaller, cheaper pack sizes.
  - Buyers are moving from traditional pharmacies to modern retail, specialty health shops and digital channels.
  - Source: [trade.gov, Burma Nutritional Supplements](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/burma-nutritional-supplements).
- **Beauty and health products sold online:** these are sold heavily through Facebook. Sellers say most items come from Thailand and China and are hard to get FDA-approved — [DMG](https://www.dmediag.com/news/online-sellers-struggle-fda-certificate.html); [Frontier Myanmar](https://www.frontiermyanmar.net/en/?p=3069). FDA-banned steroid-containing cosmetics were still widely sold in Rakhine State in 2023 — [DMG](https://dmediag.com/news/fda-checfood.html).
- **Gyms:** Yangon business-district gyms are estimated at about 113,820 MMK a month (Expatistan, only 8 price points, "only a guess") — [Expatistan](https://expatistan.com/price/gym/yangon). CityCost (Oct 2026, AI plus user input) gives about 40,500 MMK on average, with a range of roughly $9–$129 — [CityCost](https://citycost.org/yangon/). In 2019, expat-oriented gyms such as Powerhouse and Balance Fitness charged $100–150 a month — [Talesmag post report](https://talesmag.com/real-post-report-city-question-answers/1099/51). These figures suggest gyms are mainly an urban middle- and upper-class habit.

### Inferences
- Diabetes, prediabetes and hypertension together probably affect 30%+ of adults (the conditions overlap). This is a large group with a medical reason to count rice and sugar. The likely buyer is often a family member, such as an adult child in Yangon, rather than the older patient. *(Inference: there is no source on who pays.)*
- Women's interest in weight and appearance is visible in the strength of the beauty and supplement market and in Facebook social commerce. No source measures weight-loss intent directly.
- Supplement buyers prefer small packs. That fits a short, prepaid pass better than an annual plan.
- Gym members are a small, high-income urban group. They will pay, but there are few of them.

### Gaps
- I found no survey of Myanmar consumers' weight-loss intent, body-image attitudes, or diet or fitness behaviour by age, sex, urban/rural or income. I also found no Nielsen, Kantar or MMRD summary.
- I could not retrieve national overweight and obesity prevalence from STEPS 2014 or DHS 2015–16. The figure of about 22% overweight that circulates is unverified.
- I found no data on men's fitness (gym demographics, protein-supplement sales), and no post-2021 NCD survey.
- I did not search for slimming-product prices or dietitian consultation fees in Myanmar.

## 2. Health-information behaviour: sources, trust, language and voice

### Takeaway
Facebook is the main channel for health content and health-product sales in Myanmar. It carries useful health information and misinformation side by side. Burmese-language health content is limited and trust in official sources is low. Zawgyi was still dominant on phones as late as 2019, when the switch to Unicode was mandated, and I found no current adoption figure. I found no study on voice-message habits or on trust in health apps.

### Cited Findings
- **Northern Myanmar (ERIA, 2020):** research on how people use ICT to find health information found that Burmese-language health content is limited and trust in official sources is low — [ERIA Chapter 14, 2020](https://www.eria.org/uploads/media/Books/2020-E-commerce-Connectivity-in-ASEAN/18_Chapter-14-Connectivity-and-the-Healthcare-Market-in-Myanmar.pdf).
- **Facebook nutrition page:** a nutrition-education page in Myanmar reached about 2.6 million people, mostly organically. The authors argue social media can reach families where conflict limits traditional outreach. The junta has restricted Facebook since the 2021 coup — [Proceedings of the Nutrition Society, Dec 2024](https://resolve.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-nutrition-society/article/use-of-social-media-for-promoting-nutrition-in-a-conflictaffected-country-facebook-insights/C923EF9671FB3E7F6E5050C8D2D374F8).
- **COVID-19 on Facebook:** large Facebook groups carried authoritative Ministry of Health information alongside misleading health content — [Democracy Reporting International, 2020](https://democracy-reporting.org/en/office/myanmar/publications/covid-19-misinformation-in-myanmar-facebook-groups-and-discrimination-of-minorities).
- **Young men in Mandalay:** Facebook was often their first source of sexual-health information (qualitative study, n=15) — [Mahidol repository, 2019](https://repository.li.mahidol.ac.th/handle/123456789/92154).
- **Wellness sales:** Facebook and Telegram drive supplement and wellness sales, and buyers are moving away from traditional pharmacies — [trade.gov 2026](https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/burma-nutritional-supplements).
- **Zawgyi versus Unicode:**
  - Around late 2019, an official cited about 1 million Unicode users against 8.5 million Zawgyi users (about 85% Zawgyi) — [Code War, Inquirer/AFP](https://technology.inquirer.net/?p=90985).
  - Other sources say over 90% of devices used Zawgyi — [ASEAN Post](https://theaseanpost.com/zawgyi).
  - Some Chinese Android phones sold in Myanmar shipped with Zawgyi as the main font — [Global Voices, 2019](https://rising.globalvoices.org/blog/2019/09/06/unified-under-one-font-system-as-myanmar-prepares-to-migrate-from-zawgyi-to-unicode/).
  - An estimated 10–15% of mainly older phones could not convert — [Egypt Independent/AFP](https://www.egyptindependent.com/code-war-myanmars-final-digital-battle-ends/).
  - Government, telecoms, banks and media were legally required to move to Unicode — [ASEAN Post](https://theaseanpost.com/node/5147).
  - Dimagi's field notes for Android apps in Myanmar describe handling both encodings — [Dimagi wiki](https://dimagi.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/commcarepublic/pages/2143949112/Myanmar+Android+Zawgyi+and+Unicode).

### Inferences
- Facebook is where people find and buy health products, so acquisition and trust-building for Sar AI will probably run through Burmese Facebook pages and groups, plus Telegram.
- Trust in official sources is low and FDA-banned products still circulate. Showing "illustrative, not verified" nutrition numbers honestly may set the app apart, but its trust may have to be borrowed from doctors or dietitians on Facebook. *(Inference.)*
- The app should render Unicode. Users on cheap, older Android phones may still type in Zawgyi, so text and search input should handle both encodings (detect and convert Zawgyi). The 2019 data is old and current shares are unknown.

### Gaps
- I found no study that ranks advice sources (doctors, pharmacists, traditional medicine, YouTube, TikTok) for diet or weight.
- I found no data on trust in health apps or on current Zawgyi versus Unicode device share in 2025–2026.
- I found no data on voice-message use (Viber, Messenger voice notes) or literacy by age or region. The prior that voice input suits low-literacy users is plausible but unsourced.

## 3. Ability and willingness to pay at 4,900 MMK a month

### Takeaway
At the official rate, 4,900 MMK is about $2.33, close to Cal AI's reported annual-plan price per month. At the street rate it is about $1.05. Locally it equals about 63% of one day's legal minimum wage, about 2.4% of a minimum-wage month, and roughly 1.8 times a 30-day 1.2 GB MPT data pack. It is a fraction of a Yangon gym month. The price is plausible for salaried urban workers and families managing diabetes. It is a stretch for minimum-wage earners, and inflation of about 30% will erode the price in real terms.

### Cited Findings
**Price conversions (my arithmetic, using the rates in the header):**

| | Official (2,100) | CBM market (3,660) | Street (~4,650) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4,900 MMK (1 month) | $2.33 | $1.34 | $1.05 |
| 12,900 MMK (3 months) | $6.14 | $3.52 | $2.77 |

The 3-month pass works out to 4,300 MMK a month, a 12% discount. Rates: [CBM](https://forex.cbm.gov.mm/index.php/fxrate), [MoeMaKa](https://moemaka.net/eng/2026/10/myanmar-kyat-depreciates-amid-growing-instability-raising-concerns-over-rising-consumer-prices/).

**Incomes:**
- **Minimum wage:** 7,800 MMK a day (4,800 base plus 3,000 allowance) from 1 Oct 2025 under Notification 1/2025. It excludes businesses with fewer than 10 workers. Government day labourers get the same total — [Allen & Gledhill](https://www.allenandgledhill.com/mm/publication/articles/31420/new-additional-allowance-introduced-under-s-minimum-wage-framework); [Tilleke & Gibbins](https://www.tilleke.com/insights/myanmar-sets-new-minimum-wage); [WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/salary/minimum-wage/minimum-wages-news/2025/minimum-wage-increased-in-myanmar-from-01-october-2025-december-31-2025).
- **Inflation:** about 30% in 2025 (country-data estimates, not official) — [laenderdaten.info](https://www.laenderdaten.info/Asien/Myanmar/inflationsraten.php).
- **Civil servants:** the junta added monthly allowances, which a Mon State headmaster called insufficient against prices (article undated) — [UCA News](https://www.ucanews.com/news/civil-servants-pay-hike-sparks-uproar-in-myanmar/105902). The regime also denied a rumoured 40,000 MMK raise — [DMG](https://www.dmediag.com/news/cv-sv-slr.html).
- **Salaried professional:** a civil engineer with a bachelor's degree in Yangon earns about $387 a month basic gross (posting dated Dec 2025) — [Paylab Myanmar](https://www.paylab.com/mm/salaries-in-country?lang=en). Paylab does not say which exchange rate it uses.

**What people already pay:**
- **Mobile data (MPT):**
  - 30-day 1,200 MB pack for 2,699 MMK (undated travel guide) — [PhoneTravelWiz](https://www.phonetravelwiz.com/buying-a-sim-card-in-myanmar-guide).
  - Mahar 1-day 1,500 MB pack for 599 MMK, or 499 MMK with auto-renew (launched Sep 2024) — [MPT](https://mpt.com.mm/en/mpt-introduces-mahar-data-pack-enjoy-favorite-myanmar-movies-and-series-anytime-en/).
  - Shal Kyi 30-day pack for 989 MMK — [MPT Shal Kyi](https://www.mpt.com.mm/en/shal-kyi-data-pack-en/shal-kyi/).
  - The MPT prices are inconsistent across promotions, and I found no ATOM price list.
- **Gym:** about 40,500–113,820 MMK a month in Yangon (low-quality estimates) — [CityCost](https://citycost.org/yangon/); [Expatistan](https://expatistan.com/price/gym/yangon).

**Global app benchmarks:**
- **Cal AI:** $2.49 a month or $29.99 a year (CNBC via secondary source, Sep 2025). Some users report monthly prices up to $19.99 — [eesel.ai](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/cal-ai-pricing); [NutriScan](https://nutriscan.app/blog/posts/cal-ai-pricing-2026-monthly-yearly-premium-abc6e7b26f).
- **MyFitnessPal Premium:** $19.99 a month or $79.99 a year — [Subger](https://subger.com/en/th/service/myfitnesspal); [Nutrola (a competitor)](https://nutrola.app/en/blog/calorie-tracker-pricing-guide-free-vs-premium-comparison-2026).

### Inferences
- **Local affordability:**
  - **Against wages:** taking a minimum-wage month as 26 working days × 7,800 = about 202,800 MMK *(my assumption on working days)*, the monthly pass is 2.4% of income. That is a meaningful but not prohibitive share for a discretionary health app.
  - **Against the engineer salary:** $387 is roughly 0.8–1.8 million MMK, depending on which rate Paylab used, so 4,900 MMK is about 0.3–0.6% of it.
  - **Against data:** the pass costs about 1.8 times a 30-day 1.2 GB MPT pack, and data is the main digital spend people already make. Above data price is a real psychological hurdle for students.
- **Against global apps:** at the official rate, Sar AI costs about the same as Cal AI's annual-plan monthly equivalent ($2.50) and about 12% of MyFitnessPal's monthly price. At the street rate, it is less than half of Cal AI. Pricing in MMK at near-global USD levels is ambitious for Myanmar. It looks more reasonable once you note that most people's real purchasing power tracks the street rate.
- **Inflation:** at about 30% a year (2025 estimate), a fixed 4,900 MMK loses roughly a quarter of its real value within a year. Plan for periodic repricing, or anchor the price to a data-pack equivalent.
- **Suggested reading of the price:** about 4,900 MMK fits urban salaried workers, families with a diabetic member, and gym users. For students and minimum-wage earners, a cheaper short pass (7-day) or a top-up-style scan pack might be needed. *(Inference: not tested.)*

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2025–2026 median urban salary, civil-servant pay scale in MMK, or student allowance data. The Myanmar Living Conditions Survey and Labour Force Survey figures were not retrieved.
- I did not search for streaming or Viber subscription prices, dietitian fees, glucometer or test-strip prices, or slimming-product prices.
- I did not verify Thai App Store prices for Cal AI or MyFitnessPal (the Subger Thailand page did not show THB prices).
- The CBM 3,660 "market trading rate" came from a search summary of the CBM page and should be checked on the live page.

## 4. How Myanmar users pay for digital things, and freemium conversion

### Takeaway
Mobile wallets are mass-market: KBZPay claims more than 22 million users and Wave Money has historically dominated mobile money. That makes wallet-funded prepaid passes feasible. I found no evidence on distrust of auto-renew, but one operator datapoint is suggestive: MPT discounts its auto-renew data pack, which implies it has to pay customers to accept recurring billing. Freemium health apps globally convert only about 2–4% of users to paid, and Android users convert at about half the iOS rate. Plan revenue on low single-digit conversion.

### Cited Findings
- **KBZPay:** more than 22 million users (about 60% of adults), with over 3.8 billion transactions in 2025, up from 2.1 billion in 2024 (Huawei promotional case study, may be inflated) — [Huawei KBZPay case](https://e.huawei.com/au/case-studies/industries/finance/kbzpay). KBZ Bank's award profile cites 19 million active users in 2024 — [FinanceAsia 2025](https://www.financeasia.com/article/winner-spotlight-2025-kbz-bank/503374).
- **Wave Money (WavePay):** described as holding about 80% of the mobile financial services market (GSMA view via secondary source) — [Fintech Times 2025](https://thefintechtimes.com/?p=133703). An older 2020 figure of 1.3 million monthly users is outdated — [DigFin](https://www.digfingroup.com/?p=10161).
- **MPT auto-renew discount:** the Mahar pack costs 599 MMK, or 499 MMK with auto-renew — [MPT](https://mpt.com.mm/en/mpt-introduces-mahar-data-pack-enjoy-favorite-myanmar-movies-and-series-anytime-en/). MPT has also offered monthly data loans — [Mobile World Live](https://www.mobileworldlive.com/asia/asia-news/mpt-launches-monthly-data-loans/).
- **Global conversion benchmarks:**
  - Freemium apps have a median day-35 conversion of about 2.1%, against 10.7% for hard paywalls (RevenueCat 2026 data via secondary blog) — [Airbridge](https://www.airbridge.io/blog/hard-vs-soft-paywalls).
  - Health and fitness apps have a median trial-to-paid rate of 39.9%, and 68.3% for the top decile — [RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2025](https://revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2025).
  - Health and fitness download-to-paid medians are about 3.8–4.2% (Adapty) — [Adapty State of In-App Subscriptions 2026](https://adapty.io/state-of-in-app-subscriptions/).
  - Health and fitness conversion is about 2 times higher on iOS than on Android (secondary) — [Adapty blog](https://adapty.io/blog/freemium-to-premium-conversion-techniques).

### Inferences
- Wallet penetration is high, so paying by KBZPay or WavePay (manual top-up into a prepaid pass) is a natural fit. Recurring auto-debit is probably less accepted. MPT's discount for opting into auto-renew supports a prepaid default, but this is weak evidence.
- Sar AI is Android-first in a low-income market with a soft paywall (3 free scans a day). Expect conversion at or below global freemium medians, so roughly 1–2% of active users is a cautious planning assumption *(estimate, not sourced for Myanmar)*.
- Google Play billing availability and Play-gift-card use in Myanmar is a practical constraint I did not research. It likely matters for whether wallet payments must go outside the store.

### Gaps
- I found no Myanmar-specific evidence on distrust of subscriptions or auto-renew, Google Play billing or carrier billing availability, or paid-app conversion in Myanmar or similar markets (Cambodia, Laos, Bangladesh).
- I found no current WavePay user count, and the primary RevenueCat and Adapty reports were not fetched.

## 5. The Burmese diaspora as a segment

### Takeaway
Thailand is by far the largest diaspora market, at about 4.6 million Myanmar people (Nov 2025, IOM via press). Most work in low-wage jobs paying a Bangkok minimum of 400 THB a day, which is still several times Myanmar's wage. Japan (about 160,000–183,000) and Singapore (about 200,000 as of 2017) are smaller, higher-income pockets that can easily pay global prices. The diaspora is a credible higher-ability-to-pay segment, but I found no health data on it.

### Cited Findings
- **Thailand, size:** about 4.6 million Myanmar people as of Nov 2025, 64% registered with the Department of Employment (IOM report via press) — [Irrawaddy](https://www.irrawaddy.com/news/burma/nearly-700000-myanmar-nationals-enter-thailand-in-jan-apr-iom.html). The IOM July 2025 brief estimated 4.1 million (Dec 2023), including 2.3 million registered workers — [UN Thailand rapid assessment, Jul 2025](https://thailand.un.org/sites/default/files/2026-02/rapid-assessment_lmi_29-july-2025.pdf). IOM's 2023 annual report gives a lower 1.7 million registered workers, so definitions differ — [Asia News](https://www.asianews.it/en/south-east-asia/thailand/un-thailand-hosts-53-million-migrants-stretching-its-capacity).
- **Thailand, 2026 inflow:** nearly 700,000 Myanmar nationals entered Thailand in Jan–Apr (IOM via press) — [Irrawaddy](https://www.irrawaddy.com/news/burma/nearly-700000-myanmar-nationals-enter-thailand-in-jan-apr-iom.html); [ASEAN Now](https://aseannow.com/topic/1397899-nearly-700000-flee-myanmar-into-thailand-in-2026-surge/).
- **Thailand, wages:** the minimum wage in Bangkok was 400 THB a day from 1 Jul 2025. The lowest provincial rate is 337–338 THB, and the government targets 600 THB by 2027 — [Khaosod English](https://www.khaosodenglish.com/news/business/2025/07/01/bangkok-minimum-wage-rises-to-400-baht-benefiting-700000-workers/); [ASEAN Briefing](https://www.aseanbriefing.com/news/thailands-new-minimum-wage-for-2025-what-businesses-and-workers-need-to-know/).
- **Japan:** 160,362 Burmese residents (2025), or 182,567 people of Myanmar descent (Dec 2025). The two figures use different definitions — [Wikipedia: Burmese people in Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_people_in_Japan).
- **Singapore:** about 200,000 (2017). Nearly 300 Myanmar nationals naturalised in 2024 — [RFA, Feb 2025](https://rfa.org/english/myanmar/2025/02/04/myanmar-nationals-get-singapore-passports/).
- **Malaysia:** "some half a million" work there (undated) — [Wikipedia: Burmese diaspora](https://www.Wikipedia.com/wiki/Burmese_diaspora).
- **Citizens abroad (2020 Myanmar government data):** about 1.6 million, with Malaysia about 14%, Singapore about 4.5% and Japan about 1.8%. This is clearly an undercount next to the IOM Thailand figure — [JCSH Rangsit, 2025](https://jcsh.rsu.ac.th/files/issues/V12N1/252_20250529190852.pdf).

### Inferences
- **Thailand:** a Bangkok minimum-wage worker earns about 400 THB a day. 4,900 MMK is about $1.05–2.33. At an assumed ~33 THB per USD *(unsourced assumption)* that is about 35–77 THB, under a quarter of one day's wage. That is easily affordable compared with Myanmar earners. But the app would need to accept Thai payment rails (PromptPay, TrueMoney, Thai Play Store billing) or KBZPay or WavePay held by family. *(Inference.)*
- **Japan and Singapore:** these users can pay global prices. A USD or local-currency price tier, roughly Cal AI level ($2.50–$5 a month), may be justified there. *(Inference.)*
- **Product fit:** the diaspora eats Burmese food abroad and is under-served by Thai or English food databases. A Burmese-dish database is a differentiator for them too.

### Gaps
- I found no health or NCD data, or diet-change data, for Myanmar migrants in Thailand, Malaysia or Singapore.
- I found no data on migrant smartphone or app-payment behaviour, current Singapore or Malaysia counts, or US numbers (not searched).

## 6. Rice and meal culture details that matter for UX

### Takeaway
Myanmar has the highest per-person rice consumption in the world: about 279 kg of milled rice a year, roughly 3 times the global median. Logging rice portions accurately ("plates", "cups", "ladles") is the single most important UX detail for calories. I found no credible studies quantifying plates a day, shared meals (ဝိုင်းစား), tea-shop eating, Uposatha fasting or donation feasts (အလှူ) in the time available.

### Cited Findings
- **Rice consumption:** about 279 kg per person per year, the highest in the world, ahead of Comoros at about 275 kg — [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/rice-consumption-by-country). A FAO-based dataset gives 278.97 kg per person in 2025, about 3 times the global median of 80.5 kg — [Worldostats](https://worldostats.com/country-stats/rice-consumption-per-capita-by-country/). Earlier FAO and USDA material on Myanmar rice is also available — [FAO country profile](https://fao.org/docrep/pdf/008/a0205m/Myanmar.pdf); [USDA GAIN Grain and Feed 2014](https://data.opendevelopmentmekong.net/lo/dataset/5a444945-e050-40e4-bb57-8afbd2d79172/resource/3b94d7f4-73d7-413b-86d9-1d75354c2055/download/grain-and-feed-annualrangoonburma-union-of4-1-2014.pdf).
- **Caveat:** per-capita "consumption" figures are food-supply figures. They include waste and losses, so they overstate what people actually eat — [Worldostats](https://worldostats.com/country-stats/rice-consumption-per-capita-by-country/).

### Inferences
- 279 kg a year is about 760 g of milled rice a day, which is very roughly 1.7–2.3 kg cooked. Even after allowing for losses, a typical adult probably eats several plates a day. The rice portion picker should default to multiple plates and make adjusting portion size quick. *(My arithmetic. The ratio of cooked to raw rice and the plate size are assumptions, not sourced.)*
- High prediabetes and diabetes plus extreme rice intake is the core value proposition: helping people see and reduce rice portions. *(Inference linking sections 1 and 6.)*

### Gaps
